Install Dhenlux

Free order tracking for dry-cleaning shops. Follow the steps below. Your phone will ask a few safety questions because the app is not from the Play Store. That is normal.

This app is for Android phones.
Open this page on your Android phone, or use the web version: dhenlux-order-tracking-1.vercel.app

Step 1. Download

Download Dhenlux

The file is called dhenlux.apk. If your phone says "this type of file can harm your device", tap OK or Download anyway.

Which phone do you have?

Pick your brand and I will show where your phone keeps the install settings.

Step 2. If your phone says it is blocked

You may see "For your security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps from this source".

  1. Tap Settings on that message.
  2. Switch on Allow from this source.
  3. Press the back button, then open the downloaded file again.
Open the install settings

This button works in Chrome on many phones. If nothing happens, use the steps above, or the brand help in the box higher up.

Step 3. If Google Play Protect shows a warning

  1. Tap More details.
  2. Tap Install anyway.

If it says App blocked and offers no way to continue:

  1. Open the Google Play Store. Tap your profile picture, then Play Protect, then the settings icon.
  2. Switch off Scan apps with Play Protect.
  3. Install Dhenlux, then switch it back on.

Step 4. Install and open

Tap Install, then Open. After this, Dhenlux tells you when an update is ready, and you update from inside the app. Your orders stay.

Got the file from Xender or WhatsApp?

  1. Open your Files app and find dhenlux.apk. The name must end in .apk, not .zip.
  2. Tap it. If it opens in another app such as WPS, press and hold it, choose Open with, then pick Package installer.
  3. Then follow Steps 2 to 4 above.

Dhenlux Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

What Dhenlux stores

Dhenlux stores your orders (customer names, phone numbers, items, prices and payments) on your own phone. The developer does not receive, see or collect this information.

Google Sheets connection (optional)

If you tap "Connect Google Sheet", Dhenlux asks for one Google permission: access to files that Dhenlux itself creates in your Google Drive (the scope drive.file). Dhenlux uses it only to create a spreadsheet called "Dhenlux orders" in your own Google Drive and to update it with your orders. Your orders are sent directly from your phone to your own Google account. They are not sent to the developer or to any other party.

Dhenlux does not read your other Google Drive files, your email, your contacts or any other Google data.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Dhenlux's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other permissions

Dhenlux may ask for notifications, alarms and optional calendar access so it can remind you about pickups. This stays on your phone.

Sharing and advertising

Dhenlux does not sell or share your data, does not show ads and does not use analytics trackers.

Your choices

You can tap "Disconnect" in Dhenlux at any time, and remove Dhenlux's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app removes the orders stored on your phone. Your Google Sheet stays in your Google Drive until you delete it.

Dhenlux

Dhenlux is a free order-tracking app for dry-cleaning shops. Log each order, track every stage, set pickup alarms, tell customers on WhatsApp when clothes are ready, and always know who still owes you money.

Optionally, you can connect your own Google Sheet so your orders are kept up to date in your Google Drive.

Download the Android app

How to install

  1. Tap Download the Android app above.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If your phone says it is blocked, tap Settings and switch on Allow from this source for your browser or file app, then go back.
  3. If Google Play Protect shows a warning, tap More details, then Install anyway. The app is not from the Play Store, so Google cannot check it itself.
  4. Tap Install, then Open.

After that, Dhenlux tells you when an update is ready, and you update from inside the app.

Open the step-by-step install help (it shows the settings for your phone brand).
